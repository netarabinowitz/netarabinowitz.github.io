About Us

Matcha Cafe was created for people who love matcha and want a welcoming place to enjoy it. We focus on using quality matcha and fresh ingredients to create drinks that are simple, flavorful, and made to order. Our goal is to create a relaxed café where people can enjoy good matcha and spend time with friends.
